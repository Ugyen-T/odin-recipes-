Egg Fried rice

Egg fried rice is a dish made with cooked rice, eggs, and seasonings, typically stir-fried in a wok or pan with oil. It can be made as a simple side dish or a more complete meal by adding other ingredients like vegetables and protein. Key to making a good egg fried rice is using cold, day-old rice to prevent it from getting mushy, and prepping all ingredients beforehand due to the quick cooking time.

1 cup water, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, ½ teaspoon salt, 1 cup uncooked instant rice, 1 teaspoon vegetable oil, ½ onion, finely chopped, ½ cup sliced green beans or peas, 1 large egg, lightly beaten, and ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper

Step 1

Gather all ingredients.

Step 2

Bring water, soy sauce, and salt to a boil in a medium saucepan. Stir in instant rice and remove from heat. Cover and let stand for 5 minutes.

Step 3

Heat oil in a medium skillet or wok over medium heat. Sauté onions and green beans or peas in hot oil for 2 to 3 minutes.

Step 4

Pour in beaten egg and fry for 2 minutes, scrambling egg while it cooks.

Step 5

Add cooked rice to egg mixture; mix well. Season with pepper.

Step 6

Serve on bowl, ENJOY!!!
