Egg Fried rice

Description

Egg fried rice is a dish made with cooked rice, eggs, and seasonings, typically stir-fried in a wok or pan with oil. It can be made as a simple side dish or a more complete meal by adding other ingredients like vegetables and protein. Key to making a good egg fried rice is using cold, day-old rice to prevent it from getting mushy, and prepping all ingredients beforehand due to the quick cooking time.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water,
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce,
  • ½ teaspoon salt,
  • 1 cup uncooked instant rice,
  • 1 teaspoon vegetable oil,
  • ½ onion, finely chopped,
  • ½ cup sliced green beans or peas,
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten,
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper

Steps:

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Bring water, soy sauce, and salt to a boil in a medium saucepan. Stir in instant rice and remove from heat. Cover and let stand for 5 minutes.
  3. Heat oil in a medium skillet or wok over medium heat. Sauté onions and green beans or peas in hot oil for 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Pour in beaten egg and fry for 2 minutes, scrambling egg while it cooks.
  5. Add cooked rice to egg mixture; mix well. Season with pepper.
  6. Serve on bowl, ENJOY!!!
